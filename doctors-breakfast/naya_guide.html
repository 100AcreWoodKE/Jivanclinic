Naya is the neurotechnology division of Jivan Wellness — dedicated to brain health and cognitive performance through advanced, evidence‑based therapies. Naya brings world‑class neuro‑therapeutic care to East Africa.

Repetitive Transcranial Magnetic Stimulation (rTMS) is a non‑invasive, FDA‑approved therapy that uses gentle magnetic pulses to stimulate specific regions of the brain. The pulses pass painlessly through the scalp and skull to activate neurons in the prefrontal cortex — the area central to mood, focus, and emotional regulation.

Treatment is conducted in a comfortable outpatient setting, requires no anaesthesia or sedation, and patients remain fully awake throughout. A typical course runs over several weeks, with each session lasting only a short time.

🔬 First cleared by the U.S. FDA in 2008. Today, rTMS is drug‑free, non‑surgical, and performed without anaesthesia — a focused, in‑clinic therapy with no downtime.

Key Clinical Benefits

Non‑Invasive & Drug‑Free

No surgery, no medication, no anaesthesia. In‑clinic session with no downtime — patients return to their day immediately.

FDA‑Approved & Evidence‑Based

Cleared by the U.S. FDA since 2008 for major depressive disorder. Supported by multi‑site randomised controlled trials, with antidepressant remission rates significantly higher than placebo.

Well‑Tolerated

Minimal, temporary side effects — most commonly mild scalp sensation. Avoids systemic side effects associated with many medications.

Lasting Results

Durable outcomes — clinical studies show a majority of responders maintain benefit for months following a completed course of treatment.

Beyond depression, emerging evidence supports rTMS for a broad range of neurological and psychiatric conditions, as well as cognitive enhancement in high‑performance individuals.

Clinical Applications

rTMS is indicated or shows strong clinical evidence for:

  • Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) — including treatment‑resistant depression
  • Generalised Anxiety Disorder (GAD) — reduction of comorbid anxiety symptoms
  • Obsessive‑Compulsive Disorder (OCD) — FDA‑cleared for OCD
  • ADHD support — improved executive function and attention regulation
  • Burnout recovery & cognitive fatigue in high‑performance professionals
  • Chronic pain management — including fibromyalgia and neuropathic pain
  • Post‑Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) — emerging evidence
  • Mild cognitive impairment and age‑related cognitive decline
  • Post‑stroke aphasia and motor rehabilitation

Typical Treatment Protocol: A standard course consists of 20–30 sessions over 4–6 weeks, each session lasting 20–40 minutes. Accelerated protocols are also available, with recent studies showing comparable efficacy with fewer visits.

📖 RANZCP Professional Practice Guidelines (2024): rTMS should only be administered for illnesses where there is adequate evidence of clinical indication and effectiveness. Guidelines provide best‑practice recommendations for optimal patient outcomes.
